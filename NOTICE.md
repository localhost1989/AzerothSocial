# Azeroth Social · Authorship

**Project concept and product direction by [localhost1989](https://github.com/localhost1989).**

On 14 November 2026, localhost1989 documented a social addon for WoW Forever combining character profiles, personal walls, conversations and player reviews. For the profile, localhost1989 specified a round portrait opening into a full-length character view, faction covers and in-game cropping. Version 0.2.21 adds player-created groups at the project creator's request. The public release history records versions of this implementation.
